# Roadmap: suggested improvements

Captured 3 November 2026 after a full test pass. Ordered by expected value.

**Done 4 November 2026:** items 1 and 6 became **Check page** on the editor's Review tab; item 3 became **Changes for Jadu** (comparison with the original imported page, with Word and PDF change lists). Also added: sign-off and comments in the cloud, and read-only review links. See "Review and sign-off" in `README.md`.

1. **Accessibility nudges.** Flag images without alt text, skipped heading levels and empty link text in the block list and in the preview toolbar, since pages are headed for Jadu and the content sign-off review.
2. **Auto breadcrumb.** Generate the breadcrumb from the section and its homepage, the way the side menu, contents list and previous/next links already are.
3. **Version comparison.** Show what changed between two versions of a page (added, removed and changed blocks, text differences) before restoring.
4. **Block search and collapse-all** in the editor sidebar, for long imported pages.
5. **Duplicate a section** with all its pages, for trying two designs of the same area side by side.
6. **Link checker.** List internal links that point to no page in the project, and external links to open in a tab for checking.
7. **Save as template.** Turn any page into a starting point offered in the New page dialog.
8. **Storage guard.** Warn when browser storage passes about 4 MB of content and suggest splitting into project files or linking a folder.
9. **Export fidelity for hot-linked images.** Images typed in as links to other websites cannot be drawn into PNG or PDF exports when the CMS is opened from a file. Options: a "localise images" button that fetches through the grabber, or documenting the project-folder route.
10. **UI redesign** (see `UI-REDESIGN.md`).

See also `CLOUD-PLAN.md` for the shared, account-based version.
